CLI Speed Reader

A native macOS reader that presents coding-agent replies one word at a time, with a red focal letter held in a fixed position.

The Problem

Coding-agent replies can be long, and a terminal is a poor place to read them at speed. Your eyes have to chase the text, then hunt back through the transcript when you want the last answer again.

CLI Speed Reader is a floating macOS window that presents the latest reply one word at a time. A red focal letter stays aligned to a fixed position, so your eyes can stay still. It displays the text. It does not speak it.

Key Features

  • One word at a timeEach word appears in place with a red focal letter held in a fixed position, so your eyes do not have to track across a line
  • Connects to a sessionAttach to the most recent coding-agent conversation, or choose a specific session file
  • Assistant replies onlyOptionally skip your prompts, internal reasoning, and tool results, and read only the assistant’s replies
  • Clipboard and terminalRead text from the clipboard, or watch a terminal tab and pick up the output once it settles
  • Speed you setFrom 60 to 1,000 words per minute, remembered between launches
  • Stays on the MacNo network requests. Conversation text is kept in memory; only speed and window preferences are saved
