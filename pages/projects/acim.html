ACIM Daily Minute

A contemplative daily reader for A Course in Miracles — a short passage and lesson delivered every day, with a native app across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

The Idea

A daily practice works best when it is quiet, consistent, and always within reach. ACIM Daily Minute delivers today's passage and today's lesson without accounts, logins, or distraction — just a calm place to read, listen, and reflect each day, on whatever device is nearest.

Key Features

  • Today - The day's Daily Minute passage and the day's lesson, rendered in a calm, readable layout
  • 365-lesson Workbook - Browse the full year of lessons with jump-to-lesson navigation and per-lesson detail
  • Listen - Audio episodes with lock-screen controls and a floating mini-player across the app
  • Archive & search - A calendar browser and full-text search across the complete archive, prefetched for offline reading
  • Saved & Watched Phrases - Bookmark passages and follow study themes, with an optional alert when a phrase appears
  • Widgets, Live Activities & Apple Watch - Today's reading at a glance on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and your wrist
  • Offline first - All content cached locally; no account, no login, no server dependency
