What Ships with OTTO Version 1.0

Version 1.0 is the drums. When OTTO launches, you get the full Larry Seyer Acoustic Drum Library — every kit I have finished for the drums role — playable on as many as all four players at once. Any player can be set to Drums, so you can run up to four drummers, each with its own kit and part.

Percs, Shakers and Hands are not included in Version 1.0. They are a separate upgrade that follows once recording is done.

The Percs, Shakers & Hands Upgrade

OTTO’s four players each carry a role: Drums, Percs, Shakers or Hands. Version 1.0 fills the Drums role. This upgrade fills the other three with real recordings and patterns of their own — percussion, shakers and hands — playable on any of the four players once it is released.

  • Percs — percussion sounds and patterns for the Percs role
  • Shakers — shaker sounds and patterns for the Shakers role
  • Hands — hand-percussion sounds and patterns for the Hands role

Until the upgrade is ready, those roles use temporary stand-in sounds inside OTTO so you can still arrange and audition the full four-player layout.
