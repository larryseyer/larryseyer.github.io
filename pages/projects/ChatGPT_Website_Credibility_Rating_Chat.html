About This Transcript

This is the unedited record of a conversation with ChatGPT (OpenAI) about the credibility of JTF News.

ChatGPT initially conflated JTF News with an unrelated organization (the Jewish Task Force / jtf.org) and issued a 2/10 “very low credibility” rating based on assumptions it had never verified. It took repeated, detailed pushback — pointing to the whitepaper, the sources page, the corrections policy, the submission process, and the rule-based methodology — before ChatGPT acknowledged that JTF News is “a deterministic, rule-based system for extracting and publishing minimally sufficient, independently corroborated factual events from public data sources.”

The conversation is preserved here as a receipt — a demonstration of why rule-based, transparent fact extraction matters in an information environment where even frontier AI models default to surface-level assumptions.
