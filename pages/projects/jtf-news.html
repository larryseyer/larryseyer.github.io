JTF News

Just The Facts - AI-curated, bias-filtered news aggregation

The Problem

News today is drowning in opinion and bias. JTF News strips away the editorial layer and delivers just the facts—headlines aggregated from multiple sources and filtered by AI for factual content.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Bias Detection - Intelligent filtering that identifies and removes editorial spin
  • Multi-Source Aggregation - Headlines collected from diverse news sources for balanced coverage
  • Fact-Focused Extraction - Strips headlines down to verifiable, factual information
  • Clean Interface - Distraction-free design that puts the news first
  • Real-Time Updates - Continuous monitoring and fresh content delivery
