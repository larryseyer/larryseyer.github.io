JTF News

Just The Facts. A news aggregator that publishes its own rules, so you can check what it did to a story.

The Problem

Most headlines arrive pre-interpreted. The event is in there, but so is a decision about how you are supposed to feel about it, and at a glance the two are hard to pull apart.

JTF News collects headlines across a range of sources and strips them back to what can be verified. It does not claim to be neutral — nothing is. It claims something narrower and checkable: the rules are published, the corrections are logged in public, and the ownership of every source is stated. You can audit the working rather than take my word for it.

Key Features

  • Filtering by published ruleFraming is removed according to rules written down in the whitepaper, so the same story gets the same treatment every time
  • Many sources, ownership disclosedHeadlines gathered across the spectrum, with who owns each outlet stated on the record
  • Stripped to the verifiableWhat survives the filter is the part you could go and check yourself
  • A public correction logWhen it gets something wrong the correction is posted, not quietly edited in
  • Nothing else on the pageNo autoplay, no infinite scroll, none of the machinery built to keep you there
  • Updated continuouslySources are polled around the clock
