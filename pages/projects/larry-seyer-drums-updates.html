Larry Seyer Drums Updates

Every update to Larry Seyer Drums, The Drum DAW, newest first. Back to Larry Seyer Drums.

November 2026

  • Nov 4, 2026 — Added version 0.75 for testing on desktop and mobile ahead of the December 2026 launch.
  • Nov 4, 2026 — Added struck-zone playback: snare and tom hits play the zone of the drum they strike, blending naturally toward the next.
  • Nov 4, 2026 — Added per-energy-level kick beater choices, alternating between two recordings on every hit.
  • Nov 3, 2026 — Added drag-and-drop MIDI import: drop a MIDI file from your DAW or desktop onto a Songs lane to import it at that bar.
  • Nov 3, 2026 — Added the MIDI note map, which assigns each note in an unfamiliar drum layout to one of its drums and remembers it.
  • Nov 3, 2026 — Added VectorMorph Random, which mixes morphed and plain samples at random for natural hit-to-hit variety.
  • Nov 3, 2026 — Added drum solos that build from the current energy level up to full strength.
  • Nov 3, 2026 — Added SMPTE timecode as a sync source.
  • Nov 3, 2026 — Added playback with no audio output device: Larry Seyer Drums’ own clock keeps the transport, MIDI out and sync running.

October 2026

  • Oct 31, 2026 — Added the Larry Seyer Drums instructional video playlist, with walkthroughs added as they are finished.
  • Oct 25, 2026 — Added the Playback Speed control on the pattern editor’s Feel row.
  • Oct 24, 2026 — Added keyboard shortcuts beside every three-dot menu item, and a key to jump back to the song start from any page.
  • Oct 24, 2026 — Added a System Default output that follows the computer’s sound output.
  • Oct 22, 2026 — Added natural hit-to-hit variation for single-recording cymbals, hi-hat bells and pedals.
  • Oct 18, 2026 — Added Larry Seyer Drums’ own groove model, trained on my drum library.
  • Oct 11, 2026 — Added search to the preset menus.
  • Oct 5, 2026 — Added variable band shapes, phase options, shelf shift and full-range tilt to VectorEQ.
  • Oct 1, 2026 — Added Spectrum Grab and EQ Match to VectorEQ.
