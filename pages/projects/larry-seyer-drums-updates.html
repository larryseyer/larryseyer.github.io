Larry Seyer Drums Updates

November 2026

  • Nov 18, 2026 — Quick Pick now has a MIDI channel of its own, Off to start with: once you pick one, notes 36-45 on it choose a beat and 46-55 a player preset, and the control channel no longer answers Quick Pick, so a kick note there at the start of a song can’t change the beat.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — The pill editor now shares the song’s Undo history: every edit you make in it is a song step the moment you make it, so Undo and Redo work the same inside and outside the editor, and Cancel puts the pill back as it was when you opened it in one step.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — The keyboard shortcut layouts modeled on industry-standard DAWs are now named DAW 1 to DAW 6, each with a line under the Scheme picker describing it; each follows its program’s documented default keys, every action has a key in every layout, no shortcut sits on a key that program or your computer uses for something else, and Record now has a key in the pill editor (R in the app’s own layout).
  • Nov 18, 2026 — In the pill editor, a pill trimmed or grown from its left edge now shows its whole pattern at every speed: the bars the pill plays are numbered with its own measures, the bars it skips are shaded but still editable, and bars grown in front show as repeats you can tap to edit the bar they repeat.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — The Quick Pick factory player presets are now ten new player presets, including Hall Mallets, Snappy Kit and Tight Room, and they stay out of the Player Preset menus.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Tapping a player preset in Quick Pick now shows Loading kit… on its slot right away and dims the other slots until the kit is ready, taps made during the load are ignored, and Close works again with screen readers and the Return key.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Recalling a Player Preset now works exactly like loading a song: the kick and snare no longer stay on a synth kit you had before, the playing pill keeps its own pattern, the preset’s own mixing, reverb, compressor and EQ set that player’s mix, and a player switched on with no kit gets its kit the same way.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Names with curly apostrophes or other accented characters now show correctly on the Player Card, Quick Pick slots and EQ view chips, and in saved file names.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Energy level names now stop at 40 characters, typed or pasted, and keep Title Case in the Energy Profile editor heading, and the Energy Level window spells out Pattern wherever the word fits.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — The next pill’s first notes now play at that pill’s own speed, level and timing, and the count-in follows the pill that starts the song on each lane.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — With external clock and a sync In trim, changing the trim while playing, pressing a bar just after a start, playing past a looping pill’s end and lead-ins into bars of another meter now all keep the play bar on the right bar.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Fills and drum solos now play the same at every buffer size: a fill right after another fill plays its last notes on time, a jump back keeps the fill you pressed, a dragged fill plays every note, a drum solo rejoins where it would be when its player comes back on, the foot always closes a ringing open hi-hat, and toms strike the same way every time.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Some fills in the Upbeat Rock 150, Dynamite Funk 122 and 80s Rock Shuffle 150 styles have been rebuilt.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Tom rims now play their normal rim when toms are set to Muted, and kits whose toms were recorded only with muted mallets now play them, with their own rims, under every setting, even after a hi-hat.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — An edited kit now keeps every part of the kit it came from, including second rides, bells and chimes that no slot holds.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — A trimmed pill now stays on the same place in its pattern when you change its time signature, and a pill on bars of another meter now exports exactly what it plays.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Saving a song into a bank folder that can’t be written is now taken back, so the slot and title no longer show a song that wasn’t saved.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — An Everything restore with Replace now really keeps your own copy of any file it couldn’t bring in.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — Importing a set of banks with Add To now puts them on free bank numbers instead of over a factory bank’s number.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — With the app attached to the plug-in on phones and tablets, edits in the pill editor are now heard right away.
  • Nov 18, 2026 — A note you’re holding now always gets its note-off when its MIDI channel is switched over to Quick Pick, control or Program Change use.
