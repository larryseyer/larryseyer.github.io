    Larry Seyer Drums: Beta Tester Quick-Start Guide

    Beta

    This is a temporary guide for Larry Seyer Drums beta testers, until the tutorial videos are ready. Details may change as the beta continues.

    Temporary quick-start for TestFlight testers until the tutorial videos are ready.

    I recorded the complete Larry Seyer Acoustic Drum Library. Pat Mastelotto played the drums. Thank you for helping me test the app.

    How to use this guide

    Read Welcome and Getting Started first, then try Quick Pick and Your First Groove. After that you can jump to any chapter. Each chapter is written so you can try the feature the same day you open it.

    Use the search box and the sidebar, or the Previous / Index / Next links at the end of each chapter.

    Table of contents

    1. Welcome — what Larry Seyer Drums is, and what this beta includes
    2. Getting Started and Getting Sound — first launch, what you see, the top bar, and a “No sound?” checklist
    3. Quick Pick and Your First Groove — play in seconds, open the full app, then build your own song
    4. Players — the four player cards, presets, Fill, Solo and Lock
    5. Energy and Feel — the seven energy levels, and how a pattern changes without changing the notes
    6. Songs: Arranging — pills, the pill pool, fills, lifts, banks and playing live
    7. The Pattern Editor — edit notes, velocity, feel, and record into a pill
    8. Bringing in Grooves — import a MIDI file and generate a style
    9. Sounds and a Feature Called Morph — kits, Morph, and building your own kit (the Sounds page may still change)
    10. Mixer and Effects — the mixer, EQ, compression, reverb, delay, tape and export
    11. Tempo, Sync and MIDI — the clock, Ableton Link, playing and controlling the app from MIDI
    12. Using the Plug-in in a Host — the AUv3 plug-in, stereo versus multi-out, and how to work in a host
    13. iPhone and iPad — layouts, device power, and the load meter
    14. Keyboard Shortcuts — the essentials, including an external keyboard on iPhone and iPad
    15. Saving and Backups — Save versus Save As, banks, import and export

    17. How to Report Problems — what to send me, and the contact form to use for now

    Most of the Mac and iPad-portrait pictures are now in the chapters, captured from the installed standalone app on a Mac in portrait. A few still need a physical iPhone or iPad, a DAW host, or another pass in the app; those places still say Screenshot needed.

    What’s in this TestFlight beta

    This TestFlight build is fully unlocked. You do not need a trial, a purchase, or a serial number. That’s on purpose, so you can test the whole app.

    This beta covers:

    • the standalone app on Mac, iPad and iPhone
    • the AUv3 plug-in that comes with that app, for use in a host

    VST3, CLAP and the installer version of the AU plug-in are not in this TestFlight build.
