    Larry Seyer Drums

    Beta Tester Guide

    Beta

    This is a temporary guide for Larry Seyer Drums beta testers, until the tutorial videos are ready. Details may change as the beta continues.

    This temporary guide is for people testing Larry Seyer Drums. It will stay here until the tutorial videos are ready to take its place. Chapters are listed below; several are still stubs, clearly marked, and will be filled in as the approved text arrives.

    Use the sidebar to jump between chapters, or search from any page. You can also print a chapter: the site navigation is hidden on the printed page.

    This guide is not a store and does not include a download of the app.
