ShowSwitcher

Automated camera switching for live production.

The Problem

Multi-camera live productions require constant attention to switch between sources. ShowSwitcher automates camera transitions based on rules and triggers, freeing producers to focus on content.

Key Features

  • Rule-based automatic switching - Define conditions for automated camera transitions
  • Multiple camera input support - Handle as many sources as your production requires
  • MIDI trigger integration - Control switching with your existing MIDI hardware
  • Customizable transition effects - Create professional-looking transitions between cameras
  • Real-time preview - See all camera feeds and preview transitions before they go live
