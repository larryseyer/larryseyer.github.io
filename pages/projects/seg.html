1b · Cross-section: angled encoded field lines → shear torque CLAIMED

CLAIM (Searl / Sandberg): the AC+DC process "encodes" field lines that are inclined, not radial. On one track the lines lean upward and on the other downward. The up-pull and down-pull cancel vertically, which centers the roller, while their inclined components add tangentially. That gives a shear force on the roller face and a torque that rolls it around the plate.
PHYSICS: inclined gap flux does produce shear stress, from the Maxwell stress tensor σt = Bn·Bt/μ0 (the same mechanism as magnetic gears). In a static permanent-magnet field, though, that torque changes sign as the poles pass. Over a closed pole cycle ∮τ·dφ = 0, so it can guide or transmit motion but cannot supply net work by itself (see panel 5).

1c · Claimed electron path (dielectric layers) CLAIMED

CLAIM (Searl): the SEG is not described as overunity. The layered plate and rollers include dielectric material that is said to draw electrons in toward the center of the plate. Rotation is said to throw them outward through the plate and rollers toward the C-coils, while the plate takes in fresh electrons from the surroundings at the center. The claimed energy source is this through-flow of ambient electrons, not energy created from nothing. (Animated as cyan dots in the top-down view.)
PHYSICS:
  • Centrifugal action on electrons is real but tiny (the Stewart–Tolman effect). The radial potential is ΔV = meω²R²/(2e): 3.06×10⁻¹⁰ V at 300 rpm, R = 0.33 m (3.40×10⁻⁷ V even at 10,000 rpm).
  • Dielectrics do not carry free electrons. They polarize (bound charge) rather than conduct, so a sustained electron current through them is not expected.
  • Ambient charge supply is minute. Air holds about 10³ ions/cm³ (10⁹ /m³), so collection over 0.1 m² at about 1 m/s gives I ≈ n·e·v·A ≈ 1.60×10⁻¹¹ A (≈16 pA).
  • Electrons carry charge, not stored energy. Power = EMF × current, and that EMF must be produced by work on the rotor (induction or charge separation). The energy still comes out of the rotor's kinetic energy, so the energy balance in panel 5D applies unchanged.
  • Charge conservation: a steady current needs a closed circuit. Electrons leaving at the C-coils must return or be resupplied, and neutral air supplies only pA-scale currents.
No measurement in the open literature shows this mechanism delivering usable power. It remains a claimed, untested hypothesis.

5 · Physics check: magnetostatics & energy conservation PHYSICS

CLAIM Searl / Sandberg literaturePHYSICS Established magnetostatics / electrodynamics / thermodynamics
Encoded pole tracks guide the rollers into a stable orbit around the plate.Plausible. Forces between permanent magnets can center and guide a roller and transmit torque, as in a magnetic gear or bearing.
Inclined lines (half up, half down) give a shear torque that keeps the rollers turning.A static field gives a position-dependent torque that averages to zero per pole pitch (∮F·dl = 0, conservative field). Sustained motion needs an external drive or stored kinetic energy.
C-coils at the rim harvest electricity as rollers pass.Induction is real: EMF = −N·dΦ/dt. Drawing current creates a back-torque (Lenz), so electrical output equals kinetic energy removed from the rollers. They slow unless Pin ≥ Pelec + losses.
Not overunity: dielectrics pull ambient electrons in at the center; rotation flings them out to the C-coils.Centrifugal electron potential is about nV–µV, dielectrics don't conduct, and ambient ion currents are about pA. Any electrical power still has to come from work done on the rotor.
Self-sustaining operation.No independently verified self-running SEG has been published. The energy stored by magnetization is finite and small (5C), and motion does not replenish it.

Orbit radius 0.33 m; Ieff = 1.5·n·m·R² (orbit plus rolling spin); friction 0.3 N·m; air ca = 2×10⁻⁴ N·m·s²; coil copper loss 15% of Pelec. With Pin = 0, a spun-up rotor coasts down, and the load energy plus losses equals the initial kinetic energy. With drive on, it settles where Pin = Pload + losses, so efficiency stays below 100%.
