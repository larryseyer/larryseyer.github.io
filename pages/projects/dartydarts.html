DartyDarts

Scoring and game software for darts — the side project, and the one I want to be the best dartboard software there is.

The Idea

Darts is a game of arithmetic played by people who would rather be throwing. Somebody has to track the score, subtract from 501 under pressure, remember whose turn it is, and settle what counted as a double. The software that does this is mostly an afterthought bundled with a board, and it shows.

DartyDarts is my attempt at the version that isn't an afterthought: it talks to an electronic board over Bluetooth so throws land in the app as they happen, handles the classic games properly, and keeps real statistics instead of just a running total.

What It Does

  • Bluetooth board supportDiscovers and connects to an electronic dartboard so hits register automatically
  • The classic games301, 501, and Cricket, plus custom game definitions
  • Multi-playerTracks several players through a match, with turn handling and per-player history
  • Match statisticsResults export to CSV, so averages and trends survive past the session
  • Sound and a dark themeReadable across a room in a dim pub or garage, which is where darts actually gets played

Status

DartyDarts is a genuine side project and entirely separate from the audio work. It is unfinished, and it stays that way for now — I am returning to it once the audio products are released. It is here because it is real and it is coming, not because it is ready.
