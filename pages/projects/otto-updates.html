OTTO Updates

Every update to OTTO, The Drum DAW, newest first. Back to OTTO.

November 2026

  • Version 0.75 in testing on Mac and iPhone ahead of the December 2026 launch.
  • Drag a MIDI file from your DAW or desktop straight onto a Songs lane to import it at that bar, on Mac, Windows and iOS.
  • MIDI files from other drum layouts now open a note map that assigns each note to an OTTO drum and remembers it for next time.
  • Snare and tom hits now play the zone of the drum they strike, blending naturally toward the next, and a new Random mode adds hit-to-hit variety.
  • Drum solos now build from the current energy level up to full strength, and each energy level can choose its own snare zone and kick beater.
  • Refined Dynamics and Energy swing, with hi-hats now following velocity just like kick and snare.
  • SMPTE timecode added as a sync source, and playback keeps running even with no audio output device selected.
  • OTTO’s groove model now runs on its own built-in engine, making the app leaner, plus many stability and responsiveness improvements.

October 2026

  • Version 0.74 in testing on Mac and iPhone ahead of the December 2026 launch.
  • Song search, plus a Playback Speed control on the pattern editor’s Feel row.
  • Keyboard shortcuts shown beside every three-dot menu item, and a key to jump back to the song start from any page.
  • Export now offers both MIDI File and Audio.
  • MIDI import reads General MIDI drum maps correctly, and the Mac app’s output can follow the system sound device.
  • Cymbals, hi-hat bells and pedals now vary naturally from hit to hit instead of repeating identically.
  • New instructional video playlist — walkthroughs are added as they are finished (see the Hear It section on the OTTO page).
