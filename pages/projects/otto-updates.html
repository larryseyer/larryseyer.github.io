OTTO Updates

Every update to OTTO, The Drum DAW, newest first. Back to OTTO.

November 2026

  • Version 0.75 in testing on desktop and mobile ahead of the December 2026 launch.
  • Drag a MIDI file from your DAW or desktop straight onto a Songs lane to import it at that bar, on desktop and mobile.
  • MIDI files from other drum layouts now open a note map that assigns each note to an OTTO drum and remembers it for next time.
  • Snare and tom hits now play the zone of the drum they strike, blending naturally toward the next, and a new Random mode adds hit-to-hit variety.
  • Drum solos now build from the current energy level up to full strength, and each energy level can choose its own snare zone and kick beater.
  • Refined Dynamics and Energy swing, with hi-hats now following velocity just like kick and snare.
  • SMPTE timecode added as a sync source, and playback keeps running even with no audio output device selected.
  • OTTO’s groove model now runs on its own built-in engine, making the app leaner, plus many stability and responsiveness improvements.

October 2026

  • Version 0.74 in testing on desktop and mobile ahead of the December 2026 launch.
  • Song search, plus a Playback Speed control on the pattern editor’s Feel row.
  • Keyboard shortcuts shown beside every three-dot menu item, and a key to jump back to the song start from any page.
  • Export now offers both MIDI File and Audio.
  • MIDI import reads General MIDI drum maps correctly, and the desktop app’s output can follow the system sound device.
  • Cymbals, hi-hat bells and pedals now vary naturally from hit to hit instead of repeating identically.
  • New instructional video playlist — walkthroughs are added as they are finished (see the Hear It section on the OTTO page).

September 2026

  • Rapid development from version 0.26 through 0.72, with new builds tested on desktop and mobile every few days.
  • VectorMorph extended to crash, splash and china cymbals.
  • New per-element feel choices for the hi-hat, plus tighter timing at pattern boundaries.

August 2026

  • Song arrangement refined: pattern triggers, partial fills and bar positions now follow the Songs timeline precisely.
  • Saved energy profiles are kept with your songs, while new songs start from the factory energy levels.
  • Audio output device selection added to remote control.

July 2026

  • Audio export: render a whole song or a selected region to a stereo master or per-player stems, with loudness normalization and dither.
  • MIDI export of the full song timeline, including patterns, fills and energy changes.
  • Audio and MIDI device settings in Preferences for the standalone app, and a VectorMorph on/off preference.
  • Nearly every control is now available over OSC for remote control and automation.

June 2026

  • Introduced Vector Reverb, OTTO’s own reverb engine, with a morph control across rooms, halls, chambers and plates.
  • Reverb sources can be dragged around a stage display for realistic stereo placement.
  • Vector Compressor gained a live spectrum display behind its graph.

May 2026

  • Added Tape Color, a tape-saturation stage for the master and each player bus, with presets saved per song.
  • Player presets capture a player’s full setup for instant recall.
  • A new Sounds page lets you audition and swap samples with a single tap.

April 2026

  • The pattern editor moved into its own dedicated window, with collapsible instrument folders.
  • Redesigned player cards with new typography, per-player accent colors and clear Fill, Solo and Mute buttons.
  • A 32-output channel layout for detailed mixing in your host.
  • A full sample-library audit, with General MIDI drum files now translated cleanly to OTTO’s drum map.

March 2026

  • Each player’s color now carries through every panel, fader and knob.
  • Pinch to zoom the pattern view out to eight measures.
  • OTTO remembers each player’s kit between sessions, plus a broad round of interface polish and fixes.

February 2026

  • A full mixer with channel strips, player buses and effect returns.
  • Fill generation built on drum rudiments across seven energy levels, with an automatic fill mode at phrase boundaries.
  • Pattern library expanded to 220 grooves.
  • Presets for every effect and instrument panel, recallable over MIDI Program Change, and a compact layout for the mobile plugin.

January 2026

  • Development began on the four-player engine, each player with its own sound engine and effects chain.
  • Synthesized drum voices alongside sampled kits, and a starting library of 200 patterns.
  • A responsive interface that adapts to phone, tablet and desktop, with high-resolution display and touch support.
