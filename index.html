What I do

Three practices that continue today.

Making records tells me what software is missing. The software makes the next project faster and better. The show is where both get tested in public.

Tracking, mixing, mastering

Albums, singles, film, and television post. Pro Tools, REAPER, and Logic, with surround and immersive work when a project asks for it.

Instruments and utilities

C++ and JUCE for the plugins, Lua and JSFX inside REAPER. Built in the room where they get used, then shipped once they survive a real session.

Streams and broadcast audio

Multi-camera switching, low-latency monitoring, and the automation that lets one person run a clean show without a crew.

Now: I am supposed to be retired, but building things is in my DNA. These days that means OTTO, IDA, the Vector plugins (LOUIS), VectorSampler, Sparks of Light, JTF News, and ACIM Daily Minute on the bench, and the show going out every week.
