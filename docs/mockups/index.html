larryseyer.com — three directions

Same content in all three, so the only variable is the design. Each one is a full homepage: hero, what I do, projects, the show, the free kits, footer. Open them, scroll, resize the window.

A

Signal Chain

The page is built like a console channel strip. A fader rail down the left edge tracks your scroll position and a meter rides with it. Sections are numbered stages in a signal path. Oxide red and meter teal on slate, condensed engraved caps, engineering mono for labels.

Mood: precise, technical, credible  ·  Risk: reads more studio than musician

B

The Press

A Texas show bill. The hero is a printed poster with wood type, double rules, and a deliberate ink misregistration on the surname. Projects are ticket stubs. Indigo, brick, and ochre on paper stock with real tooth.

Mood: warm, human, unmistakably yours  ·  Risk: leans heritage, less obviously a software site

Stage Light

Mood: current, energetic, product-forward  ·  Risk: closest to a modern audio-plugin site
