Stage 01

What I do

Three practices, one bench. Each one feeds the next.

Records

Tracking, mixing, and mastering for albums, singles, and film. Pro Tools, REAPER, Logic. Surround and immersive when the project calls for it.

Software

Instruments and utilities for people who record. C++ and JUCE for the plugins, Lua and JSFX for REAPER, whatever fits for the rest.

Live production

Multi-camera streaming, low-latency broadcast audio, and the automation that keeps a one-person show running clean.
